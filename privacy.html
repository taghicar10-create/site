Privacy

Privacy policy for the TAGHI CAR mobile app.

How we handle data when you browse vehicles, calculate prices, or contact us through WhatsApp, email, or Google Maps.

TAGHI CAR lets you view cars, check bundles, read reviews, run an on-device price calculator, and contact us via WhatsApp or email. The app has no sign-in, payments, analytics, or ads SDKs.

1) Data we collect

We do not collect personal or sensitive data inside the app. If you contact us, the details you share in your email or WhatsApp message (name, dates, car choice, pickup point) are provided by you so we can respond.

2) Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage access. It only opens your chosen apps (email, WhatsApp, Google Maps) when you tap the buttons.

3) Booking by email

Your default mail app opens with a draft to reservation@taghicar.org. TAGHI CAR does not send or store this draft; it lives in your mail account and our inbox after you send it.

4) WhatsApp contact

The WhatsApp shortcut opens a chat to +212 605 845 497. The conversation remains in WhatsApp; the app cannot read or collect it.

5) Price calculator

All price calculations run locally on your device. No values leave the app.

6) Maps and location

The "Pickup points and delivery" button opens a Google Maps link to our location. TAGHI CAR does not access or track your location.

7) Third-party services

When you use WhatsApp, email, or Google Maps, their own terms and privacy policies apply. This app does not embed analytics or advertising SDKs.

8) Data retention

We store no data in the app. Messages you send us may stay in our email inbox or WhatsApp history for support and record-keeping, and are deleted or archived when no longer needed.

9) Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10) Data safety summary (Google Play)

  • Data collected by the app: None
  • Data shared by the app: None
  • Location: Not collected
  • Messages: Not collected by the app (messages are sent by you via email or WhatsApp)
  • Analytics or ads SDKs: None

11) Your rights and requests

To request access or deletion of conversations you sent us, email contact@taghicar.org. You can also delete messages directly from your mail or WhatsApp accounts.
